Selected Work

SCM 400 · Team Case Study

Cold Storage Capacity & Pricing Model

Evaluated whether Lineage's Edwardsville, KS cold-storage facility could take on a new frozen customer holding up to 28,000 pallets, and priced the account. We built an Excel occupancy model across 15 rooms against current guarantees and utilization thresholds. Without changes, peak freezer utilization would reach 107.7%. Double-stacking pallets brought it down to 77.1%, inside the 80% target. We then modeled labor effort, operating costs, and a 35% markup to set handling, initial storage, and recurring storage rates.

SCM 400 · Team Case Study

Warehouse Slotting Optimization in Python

Built a model to assign 44 automotive SKUs to 52 pick slots so pickers travel the shortest total distance. We mapped the warehouse layout and slot-to-staging distances in an Excel prototype, accounting for slot cube capacity and aisle congestion. Working with AI (Gemini), we then wrote and debugged a Python optimization model in Google Colab using the Hungarian algorithm, and checked its results against the Excel prototype.

SCM 400 · Team Case Study

Import Logistics Process Redesign

Diagnosed why a national retailer's holiday import program kept arriving at stores damaged. In the program, vendors in southern China ship 80+ SKUs to a 3PL hub in Hong Kong, which builds containers for direct delivery to 1,200 U.S. stores. We mapped the as-is process in a swimlane diagram across the buyer, vendors, 3PL, and store managers, and traced the root causes: the wrong pallet sizes, order files that didn't match the replenishment plan, and quantities lost in a database upload. Our redesigned process adds SQL checks to reconcile orders and sends load plans and correctly sized pallets to the Hong Kong hub.
